Methodology

How the Algorithm Works

A multi-stage bio-inspired pipeline for precise waterbody detection

🛰️

Water Index

Computes a spectral Water Index from Blue and Red channels (B−R)/(B+R) to highlight water pixels against land.

🧩

Texture Mapping

Generates a local variance texture map to distinguish smooth water surfaces from complex terrain features.

🦅

Cuckoo Search

Adaptive Cuckoo Search (ACS) runs 2D optimization over threshold pairs, maximizing inter-class variance with spatial scoring.

✨

Post-Processing

Morphological closing fills gaps, then small spurious components below 500px² are removed for a clean mask.
